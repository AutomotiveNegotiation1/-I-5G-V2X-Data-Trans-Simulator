
## 5G-V2X Simulators

This repository contains simulation tools related to **5G Vehicle-to-Everything (5G-V2X) communications**.

The simulators were developed with support from the research project **“Development of Autonomous Driving Service Technologies Based on Ultra-High-Speed V2X Communications,”** funded by the Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT), Republic of Korea.

### Simulator Overview

The simulators follow the **3GPP TR 38.885** system-level simulation methodology for 5G-V2X communications.

They are designed to analyze:

- Radio resource allocation in 5G-V2X networks.
- Data transmission integrity and reliability.
- System-level communication performance under V2X scenarios.
